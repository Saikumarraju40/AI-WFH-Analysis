# Work-from-Home Policy (Starter)

- The standard allowance is five approved work-from-home days per employee per calendar month.
- Requests that project above the monthly allowance are automatically rejected. This deterministic policy finding is separate from the AI reason assessment.
- Requests within the allowance proceed to manager review only when the reason passes the configured reason screen.
- A clearly unrelated reason or a reason too vague to assess is automatically rejected and shown to both employee and manager. Potentially relevant personal circumstances are not automatically treated as invalid; do not infer diagnoses.
- If the local model cannot produce a valid assessment, do not auto-reject on reason grounds; send policy-compliant requests to manager review.
- Employees provide requested dates and a brief work-related reason. Do not request diagnoses or unnecessary sensitive medical details.
- The manager retains final approval or rejection authority for requests that pass automatic screening.

This is starter policy text. Replace it with the organization's approved policy before production use.
